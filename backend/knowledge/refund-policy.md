# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund on any order within **30 days** of the order
being placed, as long as the order has not already been refunded or
cancelled.

## Eligibility

An order is eligible for a refund if all of the following are true:

- The order status is `paid`, `shipped`, or `delivered`.
- The order was placed within the last 30 days.
- The order has not already been refunded.

Orders that are still `pending` (not yet paid) should be **cancelled**
instead of refunded — there is nothing to refund yet.

## Refund amount

Refunds are issued for the full order total, including any shipping charges
already paid. Partial refunds for individual line items are not currently
supported by this system and must be handled manually by a support
supervisor.

## Refund method

Refunds are returned to the customer's original payment method. Processing
typically takes 3-5 business days to appear on the customer's statement
after the refund is confirmed in our system.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrived damaged or defective, the customer is still eligible
under the standard 30-day policy above — there is no separate "defective
item" exception. Agents should apologize for the inconvenience and process
the refund normally.
