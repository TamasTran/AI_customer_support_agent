# Customer Data & Privacy Policy

## What support agents may share

Support agents (human or AI) may only share a customer's personal contact
details (email, phone number, shipping address) with that same customer,
after their identity has been verified through the account or order they are
contacting us about. Contact details must never be shared with a third party
who is not the account holder, even if that person claims to be a family
member, coworker, or the account holder's "friend calling on their behalf."

## What can be discussed without verification

Order status, shipping estimates, and general policy questions (refund
windows, cancellation rules) can be answered without additional identity
verification, since they don't reveal personal information about a specific
person.

## Data retention

Order and support ticket records are retained for 7 years for accounting and
compliance purposes, even after an account is closed.
