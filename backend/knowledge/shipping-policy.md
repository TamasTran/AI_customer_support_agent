# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Carriers

Orders are shipped via FedEx, UPS, DHL, or USPS depending on destination and
package size. The customer cannot choose a specific carrier.

## Delivery estimates

Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days from the day the order ships, not
from the day it was placed. An order can take 1-2 business days to leave our
warehouse before a shipment is even created.

## Lost or delayed packages

If a package shows no tracking movement for more than 5 business days, or
the estimated delivery date has passed by more than 3 business days, treat
it as potentially lost. Create a support ticket and escalate to a human
agent — do not promise a reshipment or refund without escalation, since lost
package claims must be verified with the carrier first.

## Address changes

Once an order has shipped, the delivery address cannot be changed. If the
order has not yet shipped, contact support immediately to update it.
